Nagano Travel Guide: Mountain Valleys, Nakasendo Towns & Onsen

An independent route through the landscapes and historic streets of central Japan.

Nagano combines mountain scenery, former post towns and traditional hot spring villages. If you enjoy walking, heritage streets or long views of the Japanese Alps, plan the season and local transport before choosing your stops.

Four places to start

Nature · Alpine valley

Kamikochi

Take time beside the Azusa River and enjoy views toward the Northern Japan Alps. It is a managed mountain destination, not an unrestricted drive-up viewpoint.

Getting there: Use train and bus connections from Matsumoto, or a bus/taxi after parking at designated lots near Sawando or Hirayu. Private cars and motorcycles cannot enter.
Before you go: Main visitor season is generally mid-April to mid-November. Confirm opening dates and final departures.

History · Nakasendo post town

Narai-juku

Walk the preserved streets of a historic Nakasendo post town, stopping for wooden architecture and the feel of the old travel route.

Getting there: Reach Narai Station on the JR Chuo Line; local trains connect via Matsumoto or other regional rail stations.
Before you go: Walk respectfully: some buildings are private homes and small businesses.
Official travel reference ↗
Culture · Hot springs

Nozawa Onsen

Explore winding village lanes and public hot baths in a mountain community with a strong bathing tradition.

Getting there: Travel by train to Iiyama and connect by local bus. Verify schedules and bath rules before visiting.
Before you go: Year-round destination, but winter brings snow and different travel conditions.
Official travel reference ↗
Forest · Spiritual heritage

Togakushi Shrine

Follow a cedar-lined approach toward the mountain shrine complex; it rewards a slow visit more than a rushed photo stop.

Getting there: From Nagano City, check the bus route and allow time to walk.
Before you go: Snow and trail conditions vary substantially by season.
Official travel reference ↗

Plan a realistic trip

For a history-focused day, build your plan around Narai-juku. For mountains, reserve a separate day for Kamikochi. Togakushi and Nozawa Onsen are better treated as their own excursions unless your transport connections are confirmed.
