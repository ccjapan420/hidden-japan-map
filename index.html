JAPAN TRAVEL INSPIRATION

Beyond Tokyo and Kyoto: explore central Japan

Hidden Japan Map is a free English-language guide to 30 destinations across Nagano, Yamanashi and Gifu. Explore preserved post towns, mountain scenery, hot spring villages and cultural landmarks. Use the interactive map to plan a trip from home or find ideas while traveling.

Nagano: mountains, onsen and old towns

Walk the historic streets of Narai-juku, follow cedar-lined paths at Togakushi Shrine or explore the alpine landscape around Kamikochi. Some mountain roads and hiking trails are seasonal, so check access before you travel.

Yamanashi: Fuji views and forest trails

Discover spring-fed ponds at Oshino Hakkai, granite scenery in Shosenkyo Gorge and quieter lakeside views at Lake Shoji. Clear weather helps with mountain views; public transit varies outside major hubs.

Gifu: heritage villages and hot springs

Explore canals in Hida-Furukawa, traditional houses at Shirakawa-go and riverside streets in Gujo Hachiman. Visit popular villages respectfully and consider traveling early to avoid busy periods.

Planning note: This is an independent travel inspiration guide, not live transit or opening-hours data. Verify access, schedules, fees and local rules with official sources before visiting.
