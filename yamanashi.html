Yamanashi Travel Guide: Fuji Views, Spring Ponds & Mountain Gorges

Go beyond the famous Fuji photo to find villages, trails and highland landscapes.

Yamanashi offers lakeside views of Mount Fuji, spring-fed villages, forest walks and open-air bathing. Cloud cover changes Fuji visibility quickly, so build a trip around experiences you will enjoy even if the mountain stays hidden.

Four places to start

Village · Natural springs

Oshino Hakkai

Explore the network of eight famous spring-fed pools, with traditional village scenery and possible Mount Fuji views.

Getting there: From Fujisan Station, connect by local bus toward Oshino Hakkai; confirm the current timetable.
Before you go: Some areas get very crowded. Stay on marked paths and avoid private spaces.
Official travel reference ↗
Nature · Fuji Five Lakes

Lake Shoji

Choose a slower lakeside stop, with shoreline perspectives toward Mount Fuji when the weather cooperates.

Getting there: Public transport is limited compared with major towns. Check the specific bus route or plan a responsible drive.
Before you go: Early starts may improve your chance of calm scenery, but views are never guaranteed.
Official travel reference ↗
Nature · Gorge walks

Shosenkyo Gorge

Spend time among dramatic granite cliffs, streams and mountain views rather than racing from viewpoint to viewpoint.

Getting there: Reach the Kofu area first and verify seasonal bus and trail access.
Before you go: Wear suitable shoes and check closure notices after storms.
Official travel reference ↗
Onsen · Open-air baths

Hottarakashi Onsen

Take a break in hillside baths overlooking the Kofu Basin, with potential Fuji views on clear days.

Getting there: Road transport may be easier than public transport; check the operator details before setting out.
Before you go: Opening hours can depend on sunrise and season. Respect bath etiquette.
Official travel reference ↗

Plan a realistic trip

Combine Oshino Hakkai with another Fuji-area stop only after checking local travel times. Keep Kofu-area gorges and baths as a separate trip. The map helps compare locations before making transport commitments.
