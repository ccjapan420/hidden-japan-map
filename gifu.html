Gifu Travel Guide: Hida Old Towns, Mountain Onsen & Folk Villages

Explore timber-built streets and alpine gateways in the heart of Japan.

Gifu has a strong sense of place: old merchant streets, mountain valleys and rural communities. Smaller towns are often best explored on foot, but getting between them may require train and bus planning.

Four places to start

Heritage · Walkable old town

Hida-Furukawa

Follow the Seto-gawa canal past traditional white-walled buildings. It is a quieter heritage-town alternative to the busy streets of Takayama.

Getting there: Take the JR Takayama Line from Takayama to Hida-Furukawa Station; the ride is around 15 minutes, subject to schedule.
Before you go: Enjoy residential streets respectfully. Canal scenery and seasonal displays change through the year.

Heritage · Gassho-style houses

Shirakawa-go

Visit a mountain village known for steep thatched-roof farmhouses and its distinctive cultural landscape.

Getting there: Check bus and reservation arrangements from regional transport hubs before the journey.
Before you go: The village is lived in, not a theme park. Popular times can be crowded.
Official travel reference ↗
Waterways · Castle town

Gujo Hachiman

Wander the old streets and follow the water channels through this traditional riverside town.

Getting there: Check train and bus routes via Gujo before setting out; rural connections may be infrequent.
Before you go: Watch your step near waterways and respect local activity.
Official travel reference ↗
Onsen · Alpine gateway

Hirayu Onsen

Rest in a hot-spring village that makes a useful starting point for wider mountain travel.

Getting there: Use bus connections via Takayama and verify regional transfers.
Before you go: Snow, seasonal closures and bath hours can affect your plans.
Official travel reference ↗

Plan a realistic trip

An easy introductory visit is Hida-Furukawa from Takayama. Shirakawa-go and Gujo Hachiman deserve separate travel-time checks. Pair Hirayu Onsen with alpine travel only when seasonal transport is operating.
